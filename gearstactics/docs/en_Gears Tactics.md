# Gears Tactics

Gears Tactics is a fast-paced, turn-based strategy game set in the Gears of War universe.

## Gameplay in Multiworld
- Collect items to unlock skill upgrades, weapon attachments, armor pieces, and passive combat buffs.
- Advance through Act 1, Act 2, and Act 3 to defeat the Brumak, Corpser, and Hydra!
